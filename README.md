Personal portfolio website of **M. Gowtham Ram**, a project manager for social media and video content with 7+ years of experience.

**Live site:** https://mgr2026.vercel.app

## About

I lead a remote team of five across 6+ client accounts on LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube. The site shows how I work and what the results have been.

- **Results:** 10M YouTube views (twice), 1M organic views (several times), LinkedIn engagement up 85% and organic reach up 65% in six months
- **Content pipeline:** how I take work from plan to script, edit, approval and posting
- **Case studies:** managing 6+ clients, organic and YouTube growth, LinkedIn lead generation, and building a startup's social presence from zero
- **AI in my workflow:** how I use Claude and ChatGPT every day
- **Experience, skills and tools**

## Built with

- HTML and CSS, with a small amount of JavaScript for the contact pop-up
- Google Fonts (Source Serif 4 and Inter)
- Hosted on [Vercel](https://vercel.com)

## Run it locally

No build step is needed. Download the repository and open `index.html` in a browser.
